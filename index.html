Center Grading & Audit Status

Grading based on Pre/Mock Test & Test Day Assessments

How the category is calculated
  • Each scored question earns its weight when passed: Minor = 1 point, Major = 2 points. Questions where "Yes" is a problem (basement labs, mobile tower, late entry, etc.) are reverse-scored. N/A answers are dropped.
  • Audit % = points earned / points possible, separately for Pre/Mock and Test Day. Centre score = 40% Pre/Mock + 60% Test Day.
  • Score bands: 95+ Excellent, 85-94 Good, 60-84 Only in emergency, below 60 Not suitable.
  • Overrides: a "No" on the centre-recommended / future-suitability verdict gives Not suitable. 1-2 Major failures: cannot be Excellent. 3+ Major failures: cannot be better than Only in emergency.
  • Critical exam-integrity questions on the Test Day form (security & frisking, jammers, CCTV, VCO/System Manager on TCS payroll, unfair means / unauthorised entry): 1 failed = cannot be better than Only in emergency; 2 or more = Not suitable.
  • No Test Day audit yet: Provisional, or Not suitable if Pre/Mock is already below 60%.
  • Imported Test Day reports carry no Pre/Mock audit, so the score is the Test Day % alone. An appraiser rating of "Only in Emergency" caps the category and "Not Suitable" gives Not suitable.

Appraiser Allocation

Match appraisers to centres by city. The one-way distance between the appraiser's city and the centre's city can never exceed 90 km.

Max one-way distance 90 km (fixed rule)

How it works: only appraisers who have no centre yet are considered, and only for the exam selected. Distance is estimated between the two city centres (straight line × 1.25 for roads). A pairing is allowed only when this is 90 km or less; nearer appraisers are preferred and the planner re-shuffles pairings so as many centres as possible get filled. Centres or appraisers whose city is missing or not recognised are never auto-allocated. After applying, send the magic links from the Dashboard.

        Pre-Test / Mock Test (20 Qs)

        1. Whether adequate display of sign boards / signages indicating the centre location, exact location of the halls/floors, Registration stations, washrooms etc are made?

        2. Location, Accessibility, and Approach: Is the test centre easily accessible from the city centre, with a clear approach from the main road, and an accurate Google Map link?

        3. Whether Separate clean washroom facilities for both ladies and gents at each floor of the Examination centre available?

        4. Whether only one secured entry point is decided to be used for the test?

        5. Ventilation, Temperature, and Ambience: Are all rooms/labs adequately lit, properly ventilated, and maintaining proper temperature control to ensure a suitable ambience for the examination?

        6. Whether any of the labs going to be used for NBEMS exams are in basement of the building?

        7. Whether the labs going to be used for NBEMS examinations are disabled friendly?

        8. Workstation Privacy and Cheating Prevention: Is there adequate physical spacing (at least 2.5 to 3 feet wide tables) and physical barriers (like wooden boards or frosted glass) between adjacent candidates to prevent peeping?

        9. Whether the Barcode Readers to scan the admit cards are available?

        10. Whether the Water Bottles (at least 500ml mineral water bottle) shall be made available to candidates on the day of test?

        11. Whether any Mobile Tower installed on roof top of the Test Centre?

        12. Computer Hardware and Infrastructure: Are there sufficient, well-maintained computer systems (functional keyboards/mice and monitors free of visual defects) with an appropriate buffer in the lab?

        13. CCTV Coverage and Surveillance: Are operational CCTV cameras adequately covering all test seats (so candidate faces can be clearly seen), labs, corridors, entry and exit points, and the Server Room?

        14. Registration and Biometrics: How many registration desks are to be used, and are they equipped with functional Face ID/Biometric devices, adequate lighting, and a white background to capture candidate images clearly?

        15. Availability of Infrastructure backup: Whether proper power backup (UPS as well as Generators), redundant network switches and technician and network administrator etc are available at the test centre.

        16. Whether centre has any control room which shall be used as central point of contact for all kinds of monitoring/supervision completion of documentation etc. on the day of examination.

        17. Whether centre has firefighting equipment and emergency exit plan to attend Emergency Exit situation?

        18. Whether this centre is recommended for conduct of NBEMS Examination? If No, what are the deficiencies which in your opinion cannot be rectified before the conduct of examination?

        19. Adequate parking space at the test centre for the candidates/appraisers/Parents is available.

        20. Remarks/Suggestions:

        Test Day Assessment (25 Qs)

        1. Whether adequate signage’s available inside the Test centre during the exam day depicting the name of Exam?

        2. Entry and Exit Management: Was slot-wise staggered entry during arrival and staggered exit on completion of the examination successfully ensured?

        3. Whether Barcode readers were used to check the admit cards of candidates?

        4. Whether adequate security ,frisking(body pat as well as HHMD) available at the examination centre?

        5. Identity & Biometric Verification: Was rigorous identity and biometric verification (Face ID/Fingerprint) completed for all candidates, with appropriate exception forms filled, without causing undue delays or unjustified entry denials?

        6. Whether late entry was permitted?

        7. Staffing and Invigilation: Were adequate human resources, including security staff (with female enclosures), test supervisors, and invigilators, deployed at the centre as per the required criteria?

        8. Whether Jammers were installed and were functioning properly?

        9. Whether supervisor/staff were wearing identity cards/badges?

        10. CCTV Coverage and Surveillance: Were functional CCTV cameras deployed covering all sensitive areas and all test seats without any blind spots?

        11. Whether there was any seating arrangement related issues found during the Examination?

        12. Concurrent Examinations: If a concurrent exam was ongoing, was it completely isolated without any overlap of labs, LAN, or human resources?

        13. Whether the VCO & System manager deputed for exam are on regular employee of TCS on its direct payroll for the following Examination?

        14. Security, Unfair Means, and Unauthorized Access: Were unfair means (like peeping, talking, Bluetooth devices, possession of mobile phones) or the entry of any unauthorized persons strictly prevented?

        15. Whether password and activation of Drive done successfully on time?

        16. Technical Issues and Disruptions: Were there any disruptions or displacement of candidates due to technical issues (e.g., server loss, unresponsive mice/monitors, power failures, or image display errors)?

        17. Whether early exit was permitted for any candidate?

        18. Whether Candidates were provided with Water Bottles?

        19. Whether any candidate took his/her test in the Isolation Lab?

        20. Any feedback/observation shared by candidates/examinees?

        21. Whether confidential exam/candidate data has been uploaded successful after conduct of exam?

        22. Whether the Ambience inside the test centre was suitable during the conduct of Examination?

        23. Whether the post test period was uneventful?

        24. Comment regarding general suitability of the test centre for the future exams of NBEMS.
